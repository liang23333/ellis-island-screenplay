# Screenplay Treatment & Scene Breakdown: *Terra Nuova*

**Format:** Feature Film (110–115 Minutes)  
**Genre:** Historical Drama  
**Setting:** Atlantic Ocean, New York Harbor, Ellis Island, Lower Manhattan  
**Timeline:** Sunday, January 15, 1905 (from 05:45 AM to 10:30 PM)

---

## Core Characters

1. **ANTONIO MORETTI (34):** A stoic, sun-weathered citrus sharecropper from Scilla, Calabria. Desperate to escape devastating post-unification agricultural taxes and debt. Proud, protective, speaks zero English and heavy Calabrian dialect.
2. **GRAZIA MORETTI (30):** Antonio's resilient wife. Observant, fiercely protective of her children. Carrying the family's life savings ($28 in gold Italian lire) sewn into the hem of her petticoat.
3. **PASQUALE (9):** Their curious, watchful son. Has developed a chronic winter cough during the 13-day Atlantic crossing.
4. **VINCENZO MORETTI (38):** Antonio's older brother, who emigrated to New York in 1901. Works as a pick-and-shovel tunnel man on the IRT Subway and lives in a dumbbell tenement on Elizabeth Street. Waiting at Ellis Island's Kissing Post.
5. **DR. ARTHUR H. BARRON (42):** United States Public Health and Marine Hospital Service (PHMHS) examining surgeon. Dispassionate, efficient, exhausted by processing 4,000 immigrants a day, yet not cruel.
6. **INSPECTOR GEORGE MACPHERSON (50):** Veteran manifest inspector in the Great Hall. Bureaucratic, suspicious of contract labor and anarchists, vigilant against "Likely Public Charges" (LPC).

---

## Act I: The Frozen Harbor (05:45 AM – 08:15 AM)

### Scene 1: The Dark Steerage Hold (05:45 AM – 06:15 AM)
- **Time:** Pre-dawn astronomical twilight (Pitch darkness, lantern glow).
- **Setting:** Deep within the bowels of the steamship *S.S. Città di Napoli*.
- **Beat:** 800 steerage passengers wake to steam sirens and shouting sailors. Stench of vomit, carbolic wash, and wet wool. Antonio inspects Pasquale, whose eyes are red and rheumy from the sea draft. Grazia checks the sewn hem of her dress. Rumors swirl in Calabrese and Sicilian: *"If the doctors see a spot in your eye, they put you on a ship back tomorrow."*

### Scene 2: Civil Dawn in the Narrows (06:45 AM – 07:18 AM)
- **Time:** Civil Dawn (06:47 AM) transitioning to Sunrise (07:18 AM).
- **Setting:** Main weather deck of the *Città di Napoli*, entering New York Harbor.
- **Beat:** Arctic air hits the crowd like physical glass (14°F, wind chill near 0°F). Breath billows in thick white clouds. The sun cuts horizontally across the icy waters of the Upper Bay at 07:18 AM, catching the bronze robes of the Statue of Liberty through freezing sea spray. To Antonio, it looks majestic yet terrifying.
- **Conflict:** A quarantine cutter pulls alongside at Rosebank. Port health officers board. Cabin passengers are cleared in the warm first-class salon; steerage passengers are ordered back down to the icy companionways.

### Scene 3: The Open Lighter Barge (07:45 AM – 08:15 AM)
- **Time:** Bright, low morning light (08:00 AM, Sun at 7° altitude).
- **Setting:** Open-deck wooden transfer barge (*lighter*) towed by a tugboat across to Ellis Island.
- **Beat:** Violent chop sends freezing saltwater spray over the huddled families. Salt freezes instantly into a white glaze on Antonio's wool coat and Grazia's shawl. Pasquale shivers violently. The red brick and limestone towers of Ellis Island loom ahead out of the harbor fog like a fortress.

---

## Act II: The Gauntlet of the Great Hall (08:30 AM – 03:45 PM)

### Scene 4: The Baggage Hall & The Staircase of Separation (08:45 AM – 09:30 AM)
- **Setting:** Ground floor baggage room and the wide ascent stairs.
- **Beat:** Bedlam of 20 languages. Tags with manifest numbers (Sheet 14, Line 7–9) are pinned to their coats. Antonio surrenders their sole wooden crate.
- **The Staircase:** At the top of the stairs, Dr. Barron stands with a clipboard, hands in his overcoat pockets. Antonio instinctively grabs Pasquale's hand to help him up the high stairs. Dr. Barron's eyes narrow—watching the boy's heavy breathing and slight limp.

### Scene 5: The "Six-Second Physical" & The Chalk Mark (09:45 AM – 11:00 AM)
- **Time:** Mid-morning (10:00 AM, faint crescent moon rises unnoticed in the southeast).
- **Setting:** Medical examination pens at the entrance to the Registry Room.
- **Beat:** The line moves with assembly-line precision. 
  - An officer checks Antonio's scalp for favus with a blunt stick—*Pass*.
  - Grazia's throat and hands—*Pass*.
  - Pasquale approaches Dr. Barron. Barron tilts the boy's face up, flips his upper eyelid back with a cold silver buttonhook. Pasquale flinches and cries out. The conjunctiva is inflamed from the sea wind and coal dust.
  - Barron reaches into his pocket, pulls a piece of white chalk, and scrawls a jagged **"Ct"** (suspected trachoma) on the back of Pasquale's wool jacket.
  - An orderly immediately unlatches the iron gate, dragging Pasquale toward the detention pen. Grazia screams in Calabrian, grabbing her son. Guards step in with wooden batons. Antonio steps between them, his fist clenched—until an Italian-speaking clerk warns him in dialect: *"Strike an officer, and you are all on the next boat to Naples."*

### Scene 6: The Long Wait in the Iron Pens (11:30 AM – 01:30 PM)
- **Time:** Solar Noon (12:05 PM, sun at 27.8° zenith).
- **Setting:** Central benches of the Great Hall.
- **Beat:** Shafts of low winter sunlight cut through high arched windows, illuminating cigarette haze and floating dust. Grazia is inconsolable. Antonio stares at the high wire pens where Pasquale sits shivering among coughing, weeping strangers. Antonio learns from a veteran immigrant that "Ct" means deportation unless a second medical board rules it simple catarrh.

### Scene 7: The Manifest Interrogation (02:00 PM – 03:15 PM)
- **Setting:** High wooden desk of Inspector MacPherson.
- **Beat:** Antonio and Grazia face MacPherson and a weary northern Italian interpreter.
  - *Question 14:* "Who paid for your ticket?" Antonio: "I paid it myself, with my own hands."
  - *Question 15:* "Show your money." Grazia uses a hidden sewing needle to rip open the hem of her skirt, producing $28 in gold lire. MacPherson counts it; it meets the threshold.
  - *Question 21 (The Trap):* "Do you have work waiting for you in America?"
  - The interpreter leans in and whispers: *"If you say yes, contract labor violation. If you say no, LPC."*
  - Antonio looks MacPherson dead in the eye: *"No one gives me work. But I have two hands, and I will dig till the ground bleeds."*
  - MacPherson stamps their manifest sheet: **LEGAL ADMISSION CONDITIONED ON MEDICAL CLEARANCE OF MINOR CHILD.**

### Scene 8: The Hospital Hearing Room (03:30 PM – 04:15 PM)
- **Time:** Slanted afternoon amber light (03:45 PM).
- **Setting:** Ellis Island contagious disease examination clinic.
- **Beat:** Dr. Barron re-examines Pasquale under a bright gas mantle reflector with a senior ophthalmic surgeon. Antonio is allowed inside. He begs Barron—not with words, but by taking the doctor's hands and placing them against the boy's chest, demonstrating that the child's fever is from the ocean cold, not trachomatous blindness. Barron swabs the eye, examines the purulent discharge under a microscope, and confers with his colleague: *"Acute catarrhal conjunctivitis. Not granular trachoma."*
- Barron wets his thumb and deliberately rubs the white chalk mark off Pasquale's lapel.

## Act III: The City of Shadows (04:30 PM – 10:00 PM)

### Scene 9: The Stairs of Separation & The Kissing Post (04:45 AM – 05:15 PM)
- **Time:** Sunset (04:52 PM) into Civil Dusk (05:15 PM).
- **Setting:** The ground floor exit concourse.
- **Beat:** Antonio, Grazia, and Pasquale walk down the center stairway toward the Manhattan ferry. They emerge into the lower vestibule: **The Kissing Post**.
- A voice shouts Antonio's name in Scillese dialect. Vincenzo bursts through the crowd, wrapped in a heavy, coal-smudged mackinaw coat. Tears, crushed embraces, and the immediate handover of a loaf of fresh Italian bread wrapped in paper.

### Scene 10: The Manhattan Ferry & Battery Park (05:30 PM – 06:15 PM)
- **Time:** Nightfall (Deep cobalt dusk, gaslights reflecting on black water).
- **Setting:** The ferry slips at the Battery.
- **Beat:** The Manhattan skyline towers above them—not romantic, but an intimidating mountain of stone, cast iron, and glowing electric filaments. The wind shrieks through the canyon of Broadway. Horse carcasses lie frozen in snowdrifts along the gutters. Street sweepers shoveling dirty brown slush.

### Scene 11: The Walk to Mulberry Bend (06:45 PM – 08:00 PM)
- **Time:** 07:00 PM (18% Waxing Crescent Moon hangs cold in the southwest).
- **Setting:** Mulberry Street, Little Italy.
- **Beat:** Sensory overload. Pushcart braziers roasting chestnuts; barrels of salted sardines; strings of garlic; children selling newspapers in the sub-freezing gale; the elevated train roaring overhead on the Bowery, scattering orange sparks into the dark snow.
- Vincenzo warns Antonio about the local *padrone*, Don Ciccio, who runs the saloon on the corner: *"He will give you work digging the tunnel under 4th Avenue on Monday. But he takes two bits of every dollar. Say nothing. Just take the shovel."*

### Scene 12: The Dumbbell Tenement Flat (08:30 PM – 10:00 PM)
- **Time:** 09:30 PM (Moon descends toward horizon, setting at 09:38 PM).
- **Setting:** A tiny, unventilated 3-room dumbbell flat on the 5th floor of 48 Mulberry Street.
- **Beat:** 12 people live here. In the kitchen, a single kerosene lamp flickers. The cast-iron stove gives off the acrid reek of cheap bituminous coal. Grazia serves Pasquale warm water steeped with chicory and crusts of hard bread.
- Outside the narrow bedroom airshaft window, the bitter wind rattles the frozen clotheslines. Antonio looks at his hands, blistered from hauling ropes and baggage, and then at his sleeping son.
- In the distance, through the cracked glass, the faint silhouette of the Brooklyn Bridge towers against the freezing black sky.
