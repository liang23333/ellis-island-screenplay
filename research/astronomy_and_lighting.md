## Cinematic Lighting & Scene Timing Implications

### 1. Scene: The Narrows & Quarantine Inspection (06:30 AM – 07:15 AM)
- **Lighting Palette:** Desaturated slate gray, Prussian blue, fading into soft amber rim light at 07:18 AM.
- **Visual Staging:** Steerage passengers are summoned to the freezing deck as the steamship enters the Narrows between Staten Island and Brooklyn. In the pre-dawn murk (06:45 AM), the silhouette of the Statue of Liberty looms through sea smoke. As the sun breaks at 07:18 AM, low-angle winter sunlight glints off icy brass railings and frozen cables.

### 2. Scene: The Open Barge Crossing to Ellis Island (08:30 AM – 09:30 AM)
- **Lighting Palette:** High-contrast cold morning white, blinding water reflections.
- **Visual Staging:** Transferred from the Manhattan pier or anchorage onto double-decked harbor barges (*lighters*) or ferryboats. The sun sits only 12°–15° above the horizon. Backlight exposes the breath of hundreds of shivering people packed shoulder to shoulder in open gangways.

### 3. Scene: The Registry Room / Great Hall (10:30 AM – 03:30 PM)
- **Lighting Palette:** Institutional sepia and dusty daylight beams piercing high lunette windows; harsh overhead shadows.
- **Visual Staging:** Ellis Island's Great Hall (completed in 1900 with Guastavino tile arches added later in 1918; in 1905 featuring open iron railings, wooden benches, and tall arched windows). The low winter sun never climbs higher than 27.8°, meaning sunbeams slice horizontally through cigarette smoke, dust, and exhaled steam, creating cathedral-like light shafts that cut across the iron inspection pens.

### 4. Scene: The Stairs of Separation & Manhattan Ferry Slip (04:30 PM – 05:15 PM)
- **Lighting Palette:** Deep twilight amber dissolving into cold indigo (The Blue Hour).
- **Visual Staging:** Immigrants cleared for entry exit to the ferry slip just as the sun sets at 04:52 PM. The water in the slip churns with purple and petrol-blue reflections. Across the harbor, the gas lamps of Lower Manhattan flicker on.

### 5. Scene: Arrival at Mulberry Bend, Little Italy (06:00 PM – 08:30 PM)
- **Lighting Palette:** Pitch black streets punctuated by yellow gas streetlamps, red-hot brazier coals, and dim kerosene lanterns in tenement windows.
- **Visual Staging:** The 18% waxing crescent moon hangs high in the southwestern sky before setting at 09:38 PM. The narrow streets between 5-story tenements are cloaked in heavy gloom, with steam pouring from sewer grates and pushcart vendors huddled around smoking charcoal pans.
