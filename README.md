# Ellis Island Screenplay Project: *Terra Nuova* (circa January 1905)

## Overview
This repository serves as the central development workspace and historical research archive for a feature-length historical drama screenplay centered on Southern Italian immigrants passing through Ellis Island into New York City in January 1905.

## Screenplay Logline
> In the dead of a brutal New York winter in January 1905, an impoverished citrus farmer from Calabria and his young family brave transatlantic steerage and the gauntlet of Ellis Island's medical and legal inspectors, fighting to stay together when an inspector's chalk mark threatens immediate deportation.

## Key Historical Constants (January 15, 1905)

- **Date of Scene Setting:** Sunday, January 15, 1905 (Arrival of S.S. *Città di Napoli* into New York Harbor).
- **Sun & Moon (NYC):** 
  - Civil Dawn: 06:47 AM EST
  - Sunrise: 07:18 AM EST
  - Sunset: 04:52 PM EST
  - Civil Dusk: 05:23 PM EST
  - Moon: Waxing Crescent (18% illumination), sets at 09:38 PM EST.
- **Weather Baseline:**
  - Severe winter freeze between two historic blizzards (Jan 4–5 and Jan 24–25, 1905).
  - Harbor temperature: 18°F–24°F (-8°C to -4°C); biting north-northwest winds; floating ice floes in the Upper Bay; sea-spray glaze on barge decks.
- **Regulatory Framework:**
  - Immigration Act of March 3, 1903 ($2 head tax, 29 manifest questions, exclusion of epileptics, insane, anarchists, and beggars).
  - Alien Contract Labor Law (Foran Act of 1885).
  - U.S. Public Health and Marine Hospital Service medical inspection guidelines.
